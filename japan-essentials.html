The BuxxCo library follows strict 1G1R curation: one game, one ROM, no duplicates. When a game was released in English, the localized version takes priority and the Japanese version is excluded. For most Japan-only titles, this means they don't make the cut either. The vast majority are text-heavy RPGs, licensed anime games, mahjong simulators, and niche releases that are inaccessible or unplayable without fluency in Japanese. Including them all would add thousands of entries that clutter the library without adding value.

These 185 titles are the ones worth keeping. Games that were never officially translated but earned their place through critical acclaim, historical significance, or the fact that they play perfectly without understanding a word. Many have acclaimed fan translations. Others are shmups, fighters, and action games with no language barrier at all. A few are simply legendary, the kind of games that defined studios and genres before those studios ever looked West.
